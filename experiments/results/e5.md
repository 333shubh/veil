Load: 1000 simulated Indian homes (`src/load.ts`, checked against CEEW and iAWE in load-validation.md), 7 summer days at one sample every 10 s; mean 10.1 kWh per home per day.

Attack: for 40 target homes with a water pump, score each 5-minute window for the target's pump switching on (a rise into the pump band that falls back 30 s to 3.5 minutes later). The generic attacker uses 500-900 W, which also catches mixer-grinders and other loads; the informed attacker knows the target pump's wattage to within 80 W. 358 windows with a pump start against 358 without, at the same time of day on other days. AUC 0.5 is chance; "accuracy" is the best balanced accuracy over thresholds.
