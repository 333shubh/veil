## Graph structure

The final set F must give each member t neighbours in F, so losing meters can cascade. On the random Harary graph neighbours of neighbours overlap, so a dense hole spreads round the ring; a random circulant graph (same shuffled ring, k/2 random offsets instead of the nearest k/2) has the same degree and the same uniformly random neighbourhoods without that overlap. k = 54, t = 33, 40 trials per cell; reporters kept in F, computed with the coordinator's own `finalSet`. "Targeted" drops 22 of one meter's 54 neighbours, just enough to leave it out.

| graph | 20% dropout | 25% dropout | 30% dropout | 35% dropout | targeted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| random Harary (used) | 97.7% | 78.5% | 43.8% | 11.0% | 0.0% |
| random circulant | 100.0% | 88.0% | 38.9% | 2.7% | 99.4% |

Veil keeps the random Harary graph because E7's exact partition bound is derived for it; the circulant graph would need its own bound.
