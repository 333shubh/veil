Veil masking is `Meter.report` (self-mask, k pair masks, report MAC). The whole round adds the meter's confirmation (correction, t-of-n shares of the round's self-mask, neighbour MACs, one Ed25519 co-signature), its check (a MAC of the active and final sets to each neighbour) and its release (its own round secret). No meter drops, so no removal, escrow or unmask. Paillier encryption is c = (1 + m n) r^n mod n^2 with OpenSSL modular exponentiation. Masking and encryption are timed in turn within each run, in one process on one core.
