Veil masking is `Meter.report` (self-mask, k pair masks, report MAC). The whole round adds the meter's confirmation (correction, t-of-n shares of the round's self-mask, neighbour MACs, one Ed25519 co-signature) and its release. Paillier encryption is c = (1 + m n) r^n mod n^2 with OpenSSL modular exponentiation. Masking and encryption are timed in turn within each run, in one process on one core.
