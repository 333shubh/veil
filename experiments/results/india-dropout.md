Data: CEEW, "High frequency smart meter data from two districts in India (Mathura and Bareilly)", Harvard Dataverse, doi:10.7910/DVN/GOCHJH, CC0; 2020 files, one reading every 3 minutes. A reading counts only if the supply was on (voltage at least 50 V); meters with under 30 days of data are left out.

Dropout within an epoch is what E7's dropout rate means: the epoch's roster is the meters reporting at its first slot, and each later slot where at least 3 of them report counts the share of the roster that is missing. Power cuts make this bursty, so the tail matters as much as the mean.

Design point: dropout 10% (`src/params.ts`), against a measured daily-epoch mean of 8.9% and 6.9%. The p90 and p99 rounds lose far more because power cuts take many meters at once; E4 measures those levels directly (every published total stays exact; fewer reporters are included).
