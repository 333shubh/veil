A report alone is 32 bytes (id, round, 64-bit masked value, 16-byte tag). Paillier's report carries a 256-byte (1024-bit key) or 512-byte (2048-bit key) ciphertext instead of the 8-byte value (E1).

## What grows with N

Everything a meter sends grows with k. What it receives also grows with k, except the two set announcements, which list every meter in U and in F. Sending each meter only its neighbours' status plus the hash of U (a bitmap of k bits) would remove that; it is not implemented.

| N | active + final set as lists (bytes per meter per round) | as a neighbour bitmap and hash, k = 80 |
|---|---|---|
| 100 | 808 | 100 |
| 1000 | 8008 | 100 |
| 10000 | 80008 | 100 |
