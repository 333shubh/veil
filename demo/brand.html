Brand kit The printed-paper system

Your neighbourhood's total. Not your home's.

Veil lets a power utility read the exact total load of a neighbourhood while every home's smart-meter reading stays masked. Everything it shows is paper: printed, cut, folded or stamped.

Tokens live in demo/tokens.css; the reasoning is in docs/design.md.

The mark

A V folded from two paper strips, one face in print blue and one in pale card, with the red sun between them. The fold is the product: two halves that cancel into one total.

Clear space

Keep one strip width clear on every side. The mark sits on butter or paper; on deep blue it prints in soft ink and paper, as in the third tile. On photographs, put it on a paper chip first.

Motion: paper in your hands

Unhurried, tactile, settling. Paper is picked up, lands with a small settle, and stays put. Nothing glows, nothing floats. Scroll drives the story; with reduced motion the camera cuts and cards are simply there. Hover a row to play it.
